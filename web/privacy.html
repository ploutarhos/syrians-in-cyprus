Privacy Policy

Syrians in Cyprus
Effective date: 26 October 2026

Syrians in Cyprus is an independent community initiative operated by Ploutarhos Pantelides, also known as Alloush, administrator of the Syrians in Cyprus Facebook page. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Syrians in Cyprus website, installed web app, mobile app and marketplace.

Syrians in Cyprus is not a government service and is not affiliated with any government authority.

1. Information you can access without providing personal details

You can read the app’s information, view forms, search resources, access locations and browse contacts without creating a named account or providing your name or email address.

2. Anonymous technical identifier

When you use features that require a marketplace account session, Supabase may create a random anonymous user identifier and authentication session. The identifier connects a submitted listing, associated images and any report you submit to the account session. You do not have to provide your name, email address or password, but this identifier is still treated as personal data where it can be linked to you or your activity.

3. Marketplace information submitted by users

If you submit a marketplace advertisement, we may process information that you choose to provide, including:

  • the advertisement title and description;
  • listing type and whether an item is offered or wanted;
  • district, area, price and relevant item, vehicle or accommodation details;
  • a telephone or WhatsApp number;
  • photographs uploaded with the advertisement; and
  • the anonymous Supabase user identifier associated with the submission.

Submitted advertisements are reviewed before publication. If approved, the advertisement, photographs and contact details supplied for it become publicly visible. The posting checkbox confirms acceptance of the Marketplace Rules (including restrictions on unlawful, misleading or unauthorised content and moderation or removal); it is not a request for GDPR consent to processing personal data.

Important about photographs: uploaded listing photographs currently use a public Supabase storage bucket. A person who obtains a photograph's direct URL may be able to view it even before the advertisement is approved, and a direct URL may remain accessible until the file itself is removed, even if the listing has expired or been removed. Do not upload identity documents, financial information, private correspondence, sensitive personal information or photographs you do not have permission to share.

4. Reports about marketplace advertisements

If you use the Report listing feature, we record the advertisement's identifier, the selected reporting reason (such as suspected fraud, inappropriate content, incorrect contact details, an unavailable listing or another issue), any optional written explanation, the reporter's anonymous Supabase identifier where available, the submission date and the report's review status.

Reports are used to investigate complaints, apply Marketplace Rules, identify repeated misuse, prevent fraud and decide whether a listing should remain available. Reports are not published in the marketplace or shown to the advertiser through the reporting feature. They are available to the administrator and any authorised people or service providers who need to handle moderation and security. Please do not include identity documents, sensitive personal details or unnecessary information about other people in a report.

5. Website analytics and technical information

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general use of the app, such as visits, page views, approximate country, referring website, browser and device type. Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed not to use cookies or local storage for analytics, and not to track individual users over time through fingerprinting.

Our hosting and technical service providers may also process limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser information, request time and diagnostic or security logs, when this is necessary to deliver, protect and troubleshoot their services.

6. Local storage and installed-app data

The app may store functional information on your device, such as language preference, authentication session details and cached app files. These are used to operate the app, remember your settings and allow the installed web app or mobile app to load correctly. They are not used for advertising.

7. How information is used

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide and maintain the app and its bilingual content;
  • receive, moderate, publish and manage marketplace advertisements, and apply Marketplace Rules;
  • receive, assess and investigate listing reports, prevent fraud and address misuse;
  • prevent misuse, investigate technical problems and protect the service;
  • understand aggregate usage and improve the app; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and do not use personal information for personalised advertising.

8. Service providers and external services

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the app:

  • Supabase, which provides database, storage and anonymous authentication services;
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the public website; and
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics, which provides privacy-focused aggregate website statistics.

The app also contains links that may open third-party services, including Facebook, WhatsApp, Google Maps and official government or organisational websites. When you choose to open or contact those services, their own privacy policies and practices apply.

9. Processing outside the European Economic Area

The Supabase project's selected database region is London, United Kingdom. The United Kingdom is outside the European Economic Area (EEA), but the European Commission has adopted and renewed an adequacy decision covering transfers of personal data from the EEA to the United Kingdom under the GDPR.

Other service providers, including GitHub and Cloudflare, may process limited service or technical data outside the EEA under their own arrangements. Where transfers are subject to the GDPR, the applicable transfer rules and safeguards must be observed, such as a European Commission adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards. You may contact us for further information about the relevant providers and safeguards.

10. Legal grounds

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely, as appropriate, on Article 6(1)(b) for processing needed to provide the marketplace service you request when submitting or managing an advertisement; Article 6(1)(f) for our legitimate interests in running a safe community service, reviewing listings and reports, preventing fraud and securing the app; and Article 6(1)(c) where processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation. We consider the interests and rights of the people affected when relying on legitimate interests.

Accepting the Marketplace Rules is a condition for submitting an advertisement and does not by itself mean that you have consented to personal-data processing under the GDPR. Reporting an advertisement is voluntary, but the information submitted in the report is processed as described above.

11. Retention and deletion

Approved marketplace listings normally expire from public display after 30 days unless their status changes earlier. This does not automatically delete the listing record, related images or anonymous account information. These may be retained while reasonably needed for administration, security, fraud prevention, disputes, backups or legal obligations. Direct image URLs may remain accessible until the stored files are deleted.

Listing reports and moderation records are kept separately from public listings and may remain after a listing expires. Their retention is assessed according to whether a report remains unresolved, whether repeated abuse or a dispute needs to be investigated, relevant legal requirements and the need to protect the service. There is currently no fixed automatic deletion date for these records; you can request review, correction or deletion as described below, subject to applicable exceptions.

You may request deletion or correction of a marketplace listing and its associated information. To help us identify the correct listing, contact us using the same telephone or WhatsApp number displayed in the listing, or provide the listing title and sufficient details. Where technically identifiable, we will also delete the associated anonymous account data, subject to information that must be retained for legal or security reasons.

12. Security

Data is transmitted using HTTPS. Database access rules are intended to restrict unpublished listings and private reports. However, the image bucket used for marketplace uploads is public: files may be opened by anyone with their direct URL, including before listing approval. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid uploading unnecessary or sensitive personal information.

13. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, restriction of processing, portability where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us using the details below to exercise these rights. We may need sufficient information to verify that a request relates to your listing or report. You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

14. Children

The public information in the app may be useful to families, but marketplace advertisements and personal contact details should be submitted only by adults. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date shown above will be revised when material changes are published.

16. Contact

For privacy questions, correction or deletion requests, contact the administrator through the official Syrians in Cyprus Facebook page or the WhatsApp contact provided in the app.
